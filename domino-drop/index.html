Ready to drop?

Make ten with three blocks, or line up four fives for a Mega Match.

Place dominoes to make matches.

How to play

Move with A/D or ←/→. Rotate clockwise with W or ↑. Hold S or ↓ to drop faster. Touch buttons work the same way. P or Escape pauses.

Each falling pair separates on landing. The ghost shows where each block will settle. Match horizontally or vertically:

3 blocks = 10 pips100
Mega Match: 4+ consecutive fives1,000

Simultaneous matches earn a combo. Overlapping tiles clear only once. Two or three fives do not clear on their own. A run of four or more fives clears as one Mega Match. Each successive cascade wave increases the multiplier. The next pair waits until all cascades finish.

Speed increases gently every 20 placed pairs. If a new pair cannot enter, the round ends.
