# Letterlock — Prototype 12
Standalone word puzzle for Evenwave's Arcade.

- index.html: markup; styles.css: presentation; engine.js: pure game rules; game.js: browser controls and animations.
- words.txt: 3–8 letter, ASCII lowercase entries filtered from [dolph/dictionary popular.txt](https://github.com/dolph/dictionary/blob/master/popular.txt), a common-English subset of ENABLE and Wiktionary frequency lists. 17,906 entries. Some legitimate words may be absent. No dictionary API or account needed.
- enable-words.txt: 80,600 unique 3–8 letter words, union of the public-domain ENABLE-derived Words With Friends variant from [wordListsByLength](https://github.com/MagicOctopusUrn/wordListsByLength) and the original common word list. The variant is not the unmodified ENABLE master list. enable-long-words.txt adds 72,037 unique words of lengths 9–12 from the same ENABLE-derived source, bringing validation to 152,637 words. Generation continues to use words.txt only (5–8 letters).
- Letter weights approximate Scrabble's English distribution. On 40% of eligible non-terminal spawn batches, eight weighted random candidates are evaluated using a prefix trie and bounded eight-neighbor DFS; the best candidate favors distinct longer words involving new tiles. The other 60% are weighted random. Terminal spawns use random letters because the round ends. No hints or automatic selection.
- Starting layout embeds two different familiar 5–6 letter words on disjoint randomly routed paths, then fills the remaining starting positions randomly (32 total).
- Shift with WASD (either case), arrow keys or buttons; drag to submit, click adjacent letters then submit or press Space/Enter; typed words disabled. Board swipes are reserved for word selection.
- Each valid shift adds two letters, prioritizing alternating zipper lanes, then other opposite-edge slots, then any empty cells. No colored obstacles.
- Only manually submitted words score. They clear in place without collapsing the board. Four new letters appear: two in cleared word cells and two in vacant perimeter cells where possible, with fallback to other vacant cells. Unrecognized submissions clear the selection but do not alter the board, score, or countdown. Filling the board immediately ends the round. Scoring for lengths 3–12 is 0/100/300/900/2500/7500/22500/67500/200000/600000. Three-letter words reset the timer but award no points. Words may follow any eight-neighbor path without reusing a tile.
- Scores are stored locally using a separate letterlock-best-v12 key.
- On page load, a modal explains the rules while the dictionary loads. The board is empty and the timer and gameplay controls are inactive until Start Game is pressed. New game and Play again explicitly begin fresh rounds.
- Each round starts with a 30-second countdown. At 90, 150, 210 and 300 seconds of active play, the next countdown reset uses 25, 20, 15 and 10 seconds respectively, with 10 seconds as a permanent minimum. Valid words reset the clock, as do timeouts (which spawn four letters in random vacant spaces without removing existing tiles). Successful shifts cost two letters but do not reset the clock. No-op shifts and invalid submissions also do not reset the clock. All action animations and generation pause both the countdown and elapsed round time. Elapsed time is shown during play and frozen at game over. Each applied difficulty increase instantly replaces the timer display with a three-second notice while gameplay continues; there is no fade or layout change. A persistent score reference beside the board (below on small screens) highlights the current selected word length. The timer turns red and gently pulses at 10 seconds remaining in the 30-, 25- and 20-second phases, and at 5 seconds remaining in the 15- and 10-second phases, with reduced-motion support. The invalid-word counter is removed. A timeout preserves the selected path but cancels any active pointer gesture so an incomplete drag cannot accidentally submit.
- Click a selected tile to remove it and every later tile from the path; click the sole selected tile to clear it. Drag backtracking continues to undo the last step without submitting.

- A hidden five-second selection deadline begins at the first selected letter (pointerdown for a fresh mouse/touch gesture). Extending, backtracking or switching from click to drag cannot refresh it. Expiry clears the path, cancels the gesture and adds no penalty letters. The main clock continues. Expired gestures cannot submit on release; a new press is required.

- Game over focuses the results heading rather than Play again. Enter and Space are suppressed on both keydown and keyup after gridlock so a late submission cannot restart the game. The result remains displayed until the player explicitly clicks a restart control.
