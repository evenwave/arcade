Make room for words

Each valid shift adds four new letters, prioritizing alternating lanes on the opposite edge. If those lanes are full, other empty cells are used. An attempted shift that moves nothing costs nothing.

Drag along touching letters to submit a word, or click each letter in order and press Submit or Enter. Connect horizontally, vertically, diagonally, or turn in any direction. Each tile can be used only once per word. Typing words is disabled. Escape or × cancels your selection without a penalty.

Every submitted word of at least three letters costs four new letters. A valid word clears its selected letters, then two new letters enter the cleared spaces and two enter vacant perimeter spaces. If needed, remaining letters fill other empty cells. An invalid word clears nothing and adds four letters to random vacant cells. All other tiles stay in place.
