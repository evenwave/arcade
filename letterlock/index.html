Make room for words

Each valid shift adds up to two colored obstacles and one letter along alternating edge lanes. Three or more obstacles of the same color in a row or column automatically clear and may cascade. They earn no points.

Drag along a word to submit it, click its letters or endpoints and press Submit, or type the word and press Enter. Words must be contiguous in a straight horizontal or vertical line, in either direction. Only the selected letters disappear; nothing else moves and no new blocks appear.
